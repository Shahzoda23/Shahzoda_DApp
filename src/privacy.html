Privacy Policy

Welcome to Happy Tails! We value your privacy and want to make sure you're aware of how we handle your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information if you wish to volunteer or help:

  • Name
  • Email
  • Phone number
  • Address

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to get in touch with you for volunteering opportunities. This includes contacting you via phone, email, or post.

3. Sharing of Data

Your data will be shared with our customer service team to get in touch with you regarding volunteering opportunities.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use any cookies or tracking technologies on our website for analytics or user preferences.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion or update of the personal information you have provided.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for 24 hours to contact you regarding volunteering. After 24 hours, the data expires.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any concerns or questions about your privacy, please contact us at the email or phone number provided at the bottom of the website.
